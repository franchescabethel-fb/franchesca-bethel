Medium · ~60 words

Franchesca Bethel is a Jacksonville, Florida–based marketer, stylist and community builder. She is the founder of The Marketing Maison, a marketing and PR agency for creatives and event hosts; Monet's, her fashion and personal styling brand; and Be You For You, a women's empowerment and lifestyle community. She also creates fashion, beauty and lifestyle content and works as an independent travel advisor.

Long · ~140 words

Franchesca Bethel is a Jacksonville, Florida–based marketing strategist, fashion designer and stylist, community founder and travel advisor with an elevated, feminine point of view.

With five years in corporate e-commerce — spanning performance analysis, promotional strategy and SEO — Franchesca pairs data-driven thinking with a creative eye. She holds a B.S. in Fashion Merchandising and Retailing and an MBA in Operations and Supply Chain Management.

She founded The Marketing Maison, a marketing and PR agency helping creatives and event hosts own, build and scale their brands; Monet's, offering personal wardrobe styling, image consulting and personal shopping; and Be You For You (BYFY), a Jacksonville community hosting curated events and workshops for women. As a content creator, she has produced UGC for more than a dozen brands, including Panera Bread, Alibaba and Yeouth.
