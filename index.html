Chapter 02 · p.12 · Meet Fran

Meet Franchesca

Marketer by training, stylist by heart, community builder by calling.

I’m a Jacksonville-based creative with five years in corporate e-commerce — performance analysis, promotional strategy, SEO and the kind of client-side thinking that turns attention into revenue.

Outside of that, I’ve built a small family of ventures that each hold a piece of me: I design and style under Monet’s, help founders grow through The Marketing Maison, gather women through Be You For You, create fashion, beauty & lifestyle content, and plan trips for people who want to see the world well.
